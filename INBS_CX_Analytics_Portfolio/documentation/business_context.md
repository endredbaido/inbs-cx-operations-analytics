# Business Context

## Company

Isla Nexus Business Solutions, Inc. (INBS) is a fictional Philippine BPO company with a customer experience division operating 24/7.

- Headquarters: Ortigas Center, Pasig
- Sites: Cebu IT Park and Davao
- CX division: approximately 120 agents
- Teams: 8
- Client accounts: 9 fictional clients
- Reporting period: January–December 2025

## Business Situation

Management reported declining SLA performance and wanted to understand whether the issue was related to workload, staffing, client mix, shifts, ticket priority, resolution processes, or data-quality problems.

The project investigates operational patterns rather than claiming one proven cause.

## Main Questions

1. How did SLA performance change during 2025?
2. Which SLA component deteriorated more: first response or resolution?
3. Did resolution time increase?
4. Were patterns concentrated by team, client, shift, or priority?
5. Was workload intensity associated with SLA performance?
6. What operational areas should be investigated next?
