# Insights and Recommendations

## Key Findings

- Cleaned tickets: **24,432**
- Overall SLA: **62.51%**
- First Response SLA: **74.17%**
- Resolution SLA: **83.54%**
- Average resolution time: **969.61 minutes**
- Overall SLA declined from **70.13% in Q1 to 56.42% in Q4**.
- Average resolution time increased from **814.59 minutes in Q1 to 1,096.63 minutes in Q4**.
- Resolution SLA deteriorated more than first-response SLA.
- Workload intensity was moderately negatively associated with SLA.
- Workload intensity was moderately positively associated with resolution time.
- CL09 volume share increased during the year, but non-CL09 operations also deteriorated.
- Deterioration was broad-based across teams and shifts.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate resolution bottlenecks and approval delays.
2. Review workload intensity alongside staffing availability and backlog.
3. Examine client-specific processes and SLA requirements.
4. Evaluate the operational impact of CL09 onboarding.
5. Establish recurring data-quality monitoring.
6. Monitor SLA and resolution-time trends regularly.
